Who R1 RCM Is — Business Model, Customers & Performance

What R1 Does

R1 RCM delivers end-to-end revenue cycle management — from patient access and registration through coding, billing, denial management, and collections. The company operates as a technology-enabled services provider, embedding its teams and platforms directly into health system operations under long-term managed services agreements.

Core offerings include: physician advisory services, coding and CDI, patient access and financial clearance, claims management and denial resolution, and analytics-driven performance optimization. R1's platforms — including Phare OS and Humata Health — power automation across the revenue cycle.

Business Model

R1 operates primarily on a percentage-of-collections and managed services fee model — revenue is directly tied to the financial performance it delivers for clients. This aligns incentives and creates durable, multi-year client relationships. Typical contract lengths run 5–10 years with renewal optionality.
