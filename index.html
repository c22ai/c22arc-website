Methodology

We diagnose before we build.
We mentor after we deliver.

Most AI developers take a spec and ship code. Every C22Arc engagement follows a three-phase model that produces systems clients can actually own and scale.

01
Discover

We develop the use case from your raw problem — not what you think you need, but what you actually need. The problem is defined before a single line of code is written.

02
Define

That use case becomes structured requirements. We educate your team on what the solution must look like and why. You understand precisely what you are receiving before we build it.

03
Deliver + Mentor

We ship production software, then stay to mentor your team until they own it. You don't just receive an application — you gain the capability to own and scale it.
